**Date:** 2026-07-17 · **Auditor:** Claude (QA engineer + growth PM + product strategist lenses)
**Scope:** local dev (`localhost:3000`, commit `d7a425f`) + deployed `https://getinshape.co.in` · phone viewport 375×812 · light + dark themes
**Test accounts (throwaway, plus-addressed, admin-confirmed):** `…+qa1@` (30 days fabricated history, streak 9, weights 85→84.5 kg) · `…+qa2@` (fresh, skip-path onboarding)

> **Tooling caveat:** the audit browser could not capture screenshots or run `requestAnimationFrame` (environment limitation, not an app bug — verified by direct rAF probe). Two false alarms were caught and discarded thanks to this ("0 kcal eaten" hero number and dead paywall buttons were both rAF/toast-timing artifacts).

## Executive summary

The core product is **stronger than a typical pre-launch app**: the TDEE/BMR math is exactly right (hand-checked, two profiles), the free-tier limits are genuinely server-enforced, the token-based theming survives dark mode with zero hardcoded colors, one-tap re-log / copy-yesterday make routine logging best-in-class fast, and the test suite (151 tests) pins the right things.

The problems cluster in four places:

1. **Monetization is not live** — Razorpay keys are missing in production; every "Start Monthly" tap on getinshape.co.in fails (with an internal error string shown to the user). Two paywall bullets sell features that don't exist ("AI Weekly Insights", streak milestone celebrations), and two more sell features that are actually free (meal templates, CSV export).
2. **The app has two definitions of "a day"** — the UI groups by UTC day, the business logic by IST day. This produces a *verified, user-visible wrong-data bug* (the Trends day-diary shows the previous day's meals under the tapped date) and will misfile every log made between midnight and 5:30 AM IST.
3. **One migration was never applied** — `chat_logs` doesn't exist in the live DB, so the 10/day AI-chat limit is silently OFF (unlimited free Gemini calls on your bill).
4. **Retention mechanics are half-built** — no backfill for missed days, streaks die silently with no repair, reminders are opt-in via a buried toggle, and the "you'll reach X kg by [date]" moment is missing everywhere.

**Verdict: not launch-ready** — but the gap is ~1–2 weeks of focused work, not months. P0 list below.

## Phase 2 — Onboarding deep-dive (growth PM lens)

**Measured path (as built today):**

| Stage | Cost |
|---|---|
| Landing → "Start free" | 1 tap |
| Sign-up form | 3 fields (email + password + **confirm password**), 1 tap |
| **Email confirmation** | app-switch → inbox → find mail → tap link → **"then sign in"** |
| Re-authentication | **re-type email + password** (2 fields), 1 tap |
| Wizard 1/6 "Log a meal" | photo / describe / skip — value-first design ✅ |
| Wizard 2–6 | name (1 field) + ~7 taps; **everything else prefilled** (age 25, 5'7", 70 kg, target 65, Lose, 0.5 pace, Moderate) |
| Landing screen | dashboard with clear "Log your first meal" CTA (skip path) or already-populated (log path) ✅ |

**Time-to-first-value: ≈3–4.5 minutes realistic** (vs. <2 min best-in-class). The wizard is *not* the problem — the auth loop is: confirmation round-trip + forced re-login costs 1.5–2 minutes and is where drop-off will concentrate. (PostHog's `onboarding_step_completed` events will confirm once there's traffic.)

**What's genuinely good:** meal-log-first step 1 (value before effort — rare and correct); every wizard field prefilled; live BMI feedback + suggested targets; live target preview at 6/6; no jargon (TDEE never appears; one tasteful Mifflin-St Jeor credibility line); friendly, Indian-flavored copy throughout ("2 roti, dal, sabzi", katori examples); inclusive sex selector; ends teaching the core action.

**Gaps, in priority order:**
1. **Kill the auth loop** — disable email confirmation (or switch to OTP/magic-link that lands signed-in, or auto-create the session on confirm). Single biggest onboarding lever. Fixes P1-15.
2. **Add the projection moment at 6/6** — "You'll reach **75 kg by ~December 5** 🎯" under the calorie preview. One line of math (`gap ÷ pace`), Cal AI's signature conversion trick. Fixes P1-16.
3. Drop the confirm-password field (show/hide toggle instead).
4. Persist wizard progress (localStorage is enough) so abandoners resume at their step instead of 1/6.
5. Permission priming: after the first successful log (or day-2 return), prime notifications with a benefit-framed card — not a buried Settings toggle. Camera permission is already contextual ✅.
6. The "Log a meal" success sheet's only CTA is "Log another meal" — closing the sheet is what advances the wizard; add an explicit "Continue →".

**Abandonment behavior (verified):** navigating away mid-wizard and returning → middleware correctly forces `/onboarding`, but the wizard restarts at 1/6 with all inputs lost.

## Phase 3 — Retention audit

**Day-7/day-30 walkthrough** on 30 days of fabricated history (29 logged days, one deliberate gap, weights 85→82.7 with a plateau week): streak computed correctly (9), calendar "16 of 17 days logged" correct, weekly aggregates correct, exercise appears in Trends. The mechanics that exist, work. The problem is what *doesn't* exist.

**Hooks that bring a user back tomorrow (current):**
1. Streak flame (dashboard pill) — but dies silently, no repair (P1-3)
2. Evening push nudge with streak-save copy — well built, ~0 adoption path (P1-17)
3. First-log confetti + weight-loss milestone celebrations (one-time each)
4. 3rd-log paywall interstitial (one-time, warm)
5. WhatsApp share card on Trends (under-discovered)

**Standard hooks that are missing:** meal-time logging reminders (only one evening slot, no timing control); weigh-in day nudges; weekly recap ("you averaged X kcal, down Y kg" — natural fit for the *promised but unbuilt* AI Weekly Insights, P0-3); streak milestone badges 7/30/100 (promised on the landing page, unbuilt); re-engagement pushes for lapsed users (cron only nudges users who haven't logged *today* — a 30-day-lapsed user with a dead push subscription gets the same single nudge and nothing else); home-screen widget (TWA constraint: true native widgets need Android work beyond Bubblewrap — note for v2; rich notifications are the nearer substitute).

**Failure states walked:**
- **Over goal:** deliberately non-shaming — "X over" in ember (never red) on log + dashboard; red appears only in the retrospective Trends legend. Good. But also no *constructive* response (no "a 20-min walk covers this" nudge — cheap win given exercise logging exists).
- **Broken streak:** silent flame removal; nothing else. Worst-in-class handling of the single most emotionally-charged moment (P1-2/P1-3 make it unrepairable).
- **Weight gain / plateau:** weight page shows the delta neutrally; nothing addresses a plateau week (the fabricated plateau produced no reaction anywhere).
- **Mid-day "achievement" inflation** (P1-12) is the inverse failure: celebrating incomplete data.

**Week-3 test ("log yesterday's dinner: dal + 2 roti"):**
- For **today's** dinner: 1–2 taps via Log again / Copy yesterday — **best-in-class**, faster than MFP's meal-copy or HealthifyMe's search.
- For **yesterday's** dinner: **impossible** (P1-2). The single fastest retention fix in this report is making past days log-able.

**Reminder scheduling UI:** exists (one toggle, evening-only, no time choice). Flagged in P1-17.

## Phase 4 — Competitor gap analysis

> **Method:** live Play Store listings (en_IN) + competitor sites read on 2026-07-17 via the audit browser. Exact India *prices* are the least reliable data here (competitors A/B-test them and hide them behind app funnels); treat price cells as ranges to verify. ### What each competitor looks like today (July 2026)

**HealthifyMe ("Healthify: AI Diet & Fitness")** — 4.4★, 569K reviews, 10M+ installs, Editors' Choice, updated Jul 6 2026. The India incumbent. Current stack: **Ria** (24/7 AI nutritionist chat), **Snap** (photo → calories + *instant coaching feedback on the meal*), 100K+ food DB, steps/hydration/sleep/workouts, integrations (Google Fit, Samsung Health, HealthConnect, Garmin, Fitbit), **daily challenges**, smart-scale hardware, **community**, and — their big 2026 bet — **HealthifyRx: GLP-1 medical weight-loss plans** with doctors and coaching. Read: they're moving *up-market* (medical, human coaches, hardware); the pure self-serve tracker is their funnel, not their product. Their AI plans have historically run ~₹1,000–2,500/yr and coach plans ₹6K–20K+/yr (verify current promos).

**Cal AI** — 4.5★, 271K reviews, 1M+ installs (India listing). Photo-scanner-first, "snap & track", barcode + label reading, deficit calculator framed as a **"calorie deficit streak"**, keto/IF/bodybuilding positioning. Famous onboarding: quiz → **projected weight curve with a target date** → hard paywall (trial → ~₹2,000–3,000/yr region-dependent). It's the app your AI-scan users will compare you against.

**MyFitnessPal** — 4.5★, 2.9M reviews, 100M+ installs, ads + premium (free trial pushed at signup). 20.5M-food DB, restaurant dishes, macro/sodium/fiber goals, **water tracker**, IF tracker, workouts, **Wear OS watch app**.
